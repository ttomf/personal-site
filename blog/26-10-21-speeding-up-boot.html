Speeding up boot

2026-10-21

I love my Arch Linux notebook, but one thing always bothered me: the boot time. It took about 20 seconds, and I wanted to make it faster.

I tried several things, including disabling and masking systemd services and removing initramfs hooks. Here is what actually worked on my setup: Arch Linux on a Lenovo LOQ with RTX 5060.

These changes reduced my boot time by about 5 seconds, from 21 to 16 seconds. However, initializing amdgpu still takes about 4 seconds, and the firmware alone takes 7 seconds!

Biggest improvements

The biggest improvement came from changing how the GPU drivers are loaded during boot. In /etc/mkinitcpio.conf, I removed kms from HOOKS and cleared MODULES. 

...
# MODULES=(nvidia nvidia_modeset nvidia_uvm nvidia_drm)
MODULES=()
...
# HOOKS=(systemd autodetect microcode modconf kms keyboard sd-vconsole block filesystems fsck)
HOOKS=(systemd autodetect microcode modconf keyboard sd-vconsole block filesystems fsck)
...

I also added amdgpu to /etc/modules-load.d/amdgpu.conf so that the AMD GPU driver is loaded during userspace initialization.

This only moved GPU initialization from the initramfs to userspace, but it still reduced the overall boot time by about 4 seconds. Removing the GPU modules also made initramfs-linux.img much smaller, reducing its size from 162 MB to just 22 MB and speeding up the boot process.

Smaller improvements

Disabling NetworkManager-wait-online.service and removing the VirtualBox DKMS modules also helped. Together, these changes reduced the boot time by about 1 second.
